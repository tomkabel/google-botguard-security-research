\textbf{Behavioral Biometrics \& Sensor Telemetry} &
BeCAPTCHA (academic), reCAPTCHA v3 interaction signals, commercial biometrics in DataDome/Human Security &
Measures dynamics of human interaction: mouse kinematics (velocity, Bezier curves), scroll patterns, click-timing, touch pressure, accelerometer/gyroscope polling. Distinguishes humans from bots via motor-control patterns. &

Defender's ability to raise interaction complexity above industrial human-labor cost threshold. Labor-market arbitrage, not technology race. &

\textbf{Anonymous Attestation (PATs)} &
Apple Private Access Tokens (iOS 16+), Cloudflare/Fastly Privacy Pass, WICG Private State Token API &
Hardware-backed attestation (Secure Enclave) generates rate-limited, cryptographically signed tokens via RSA blind signatures or VOPRF. Tokens redeemed anonymously at web origins for deterministic ``humanness'' verification. &
Engineering (issuer infrastructure) + Platform (OS integration, attestation service) &
\textbf{Mixed}: Fixed (device compromise via PPI malware to obtain signed attestations) + Variable (token redemption rate limits). Token issuance rate-limited per device. &
Issuance rate limits per device; centralized attestation root of trust. &
\textbf{Vendor centralization}: Apple, Google, Microsoft control attestation root. Economic and political risk of delegating ``humanness'' to OS oligopoly. No decentralized issuer network exists. &
\yes &
\yes &

\textbf{Hardware-Anchored Determinism} &
Google DBSC, FIDO2/WebAuthn, Passkeys (Apple, Google, Microsoft) &
Cryptographic proof-of-possession of private key in TPM/Secure Enclave. DBSC periodically proves session cookie possession; FIDO2 proves credential possession. Deterministic -- key present or not. &
Engineering (protocol) + Ecosystem (browser/OS integration, authenticator distribution) &
\textbf{Fixed}: Attacker bypass via device compromise (PPI malware). Economic bypass cost = black-market price of malware infection (\$75--\$200/mo MaaS, \$1--\$350 device sale), not attestation hardness. &
User adoption friction; malware infection cost equilibrium. &
Three-fold: (a) user adoption friction, (b) inapplicable to anonymous traffic, (c) PPI malware market sets bypass cost ceiling. &
